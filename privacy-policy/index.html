PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated September 20, 2026

This privacy policy for Bump, Inc. ("Bump," "we," "us," or "our") explains what personal data we collect, why, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it when you use the Bump mobile application, our websites, and related services (together, the "Services").

Bump is a peer-to-peer network: people who have an internet connection share it with people nearby who do not, and are rewarded for doing so. Running that network means we handle some data that most apps do not — records of who connected through whom, and identifiers that stop the same person collecting a sign-up bonus twice. This policy is written to be specific about those.

Questions or concerns? Our data protection officer is reachable at privacy@bumpapp.xyz (see section 15). If you do not agree with our practices, please do not use the Services.

SUMMARY OF KEY POINTS

  • What we collect. Your Google account details, a verified phone number, device identifiers, records of the connections you make or provide, approximate location while you share or look for a hotspot, and — only when you cash out — your national tax ID (CPF or Cédula de Ciudadanía) and payout details. Section 1.
  • How the network works. When you use someone else's connection, your traffic is encrypted on your phone and exits to the internet from a Bump server, not from the sharer's connection. The sharer cannot see your traffic and we do not record which sites or apps you access. Section 2.
  • How long we keep it. Most data lives as long as your account. Connection records are kept for 12 months because Brazilian law requires it. Hashed (one-way) copies of your phone number, device ID, Google account and tax ID are kept after you delete your account so the same person cannot collect a sign-up or referral bonus again. Section 7 has the full table.
  • Who we share it with. Service providers that run our infrastructure and payouts (Google, Stripe, Yativo, Reloadly, Twilio, Shake, Datadog). We do not sell personal data. Section 5.
  • Where it is stored. In the United States, on Google Cloud. Section 6 explains the safeguards for users in Brazil and Colombia.
  • Your rights. Access, correction, portability, deletion, and objection, with the specific rights and deadlines of Brazil's LGPD and Colombia's Ley 1581 in section 10. Email privacy@bumpapp.xyz.

1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Information you give us

  • Google account. You sign in with Google. We receive the email address, display name and Google account identifier of the account you choose. There is no Bump password.
  • Profile. The username you pick, your avatar choice, and your country of residence (Brazil or Colombia).
  • Phone number. We verify a mobile number by SMS code and record whether the line is prepaid or postpaid. The number is used to make sure each person receives one sign-up bonus and to deliver mobile-data rewards.
  • Payout details (only when you cash out reward points). Your CPF (Brazil) or Cédula de Ciudadanía (Colombia), your PIX key or Colombian bank / Bre-B details, and the recipient name. Tax IDs are also used to limit how many accounts can cash out to the same person.
  • Purchases. If you buy credits, the payment is handled by Stripe (cards) or Yativo (PIX in Brazil, PSE in Colombia). We receive the amount and a transaction reference; we never receive or store your card number.
  • Support and reports. Bug reports you send through the in-app reporter (with any screenshot, log or device details you choose to attach), feedback, and reports you make about other users.
  • Chat messages. Messages between Bump users are end-to-end encrypted on your device. Our servers relay them for up to 7 days and see only the encrypted payload and delivery metadata (sender, recipient device, timestamps).

Sensitive information. We process approximate location data as described below. We do not collect health, biometric, racial, religious, political or other special-category data.

Information collected automatically

  • Device. Your Android device identifier (ANDROID_ID), a Bump-generated device ID, device model, operating system version, app version, language, and mobile carrier. The device identifier is used to make sure each handset receives one sign-up or referral bonus, and to attribute connection records to a device.
  • App integrity. When you sign in or claim a reward we ask Google Play Integrity / Firebase App Check to confirm the app is genuine and the device is not tampered with. We receive a verdict, not the underlying device data.
  • Access records. The date, time and IP address of each sign-in, and the device it came from. Brazil's Marco Civil da Internet (Art. 15) requires us to keep these.
  • Connection records. When you use another user's connection, or share yours: which device connected to which, when the session started and ended, how much data was transferred, and which Bump server carried the traffic and with which exit IP address. We do not record the websites, apps or destinations you access (see section 2).
  • Location. While you share your connection we record the approximate location of your hotspot so nearby users can find it, so you can be paid for covering a zone, and to build an anonymised coverage map. While you look for a hotspot we record your approximate location and the hotspots you could see, to map demand. Location is collected only while the app is in these modes and is stored at reduced precision in the aggregate maps. You can withhold the permission in Android settings; hotspot features then do not work.
  • Usage and diagnostics. Which features you use, crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics), and performance telemetry and server logs (Datadog). Server logs may contain your user ID and IP address.
  • Website. bumpapp.xyz uses Google Analytics to measure visits. The app does not use advertising SDKs.

All personal information that you provide to us must be true, complete, and accurate, and you must notify us of any changes.

2. HOW THE BUMP NETWORK HANDLES YOUR TRAFFIC

In Short: Traffic you send through another user's connection is encrypted on your phone and exits from a Bump server. The sharer cannot read it, and we do not log where it goes.

When you connect to a hotspot shared by another Bump user (over Wi-Fi Direct or Bluetooth), the app opens an encrypted VPN tunnel from your phone to a VPN server operated by Bump. The sharer's phone only relays that encrypted tunnel; it cannot see the content or destinations of your traffic, and your traffic does not appear to come from the sharer's IP address — it exits to the internet from our server. Likewise, when you share your connection, the traffic of the people using it passes through your phone encrypted, and is never sent to the internet from your own address.

On our VPN servers we keep the connection records described in section 1 (which device, which server, when, how much data, exit IP) for 12 months, because Brazilian law requires providers of internet connection to keep connection records for at least one year and to be able to answer a court order with them. We do not keep records of the applications, websites or services you access through the tunnel. Under Marco Civil Art. 16 we would only ever do so with your prior, separate consent, and we have no plans to ask for it.

Every access to those records by our staff is itself logged (who looked, at whose records, and why), and records are disclosed only in response to a valid legal order or as required by law.

3. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

  • To run your account — sign you in, show your profile, and keep your credits, reward points and history.
  • To operate the network — match you with nearby hotspots, carry your traffic, account for the data each side used, and pay rewards to sharers.
  • To pay you — process cash-outs to PIX or Colombian bank accounts, and mobile-data rewards to your carrier.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse — enforce one sign-up bonus and one referral bonus per person, device, phone number and tax ID; detect tampered apps; investigate abuse reports.
  • To meet legal obligations — keep the access and connection records required by Marco Civil, answer lawful requests, and keep the accounting records that payouts require.
  • To improve the Services — understand which features are used, where coverage is needed, and diagnose crashes.
  • To communicate with you — respond to support requests and send service notices.

8. WHAT WE KEEP AFTER YOU DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT

You can delete your account at any time from Settings. Deletion removes your Google sign-in from Bump, deletes your phone number and raw device identifier, deletes the per-second location samples from your sessions, and anonymises your profile: your email, name and Google account identifier are removed and your username is replaced with a placeholder, so nothing in the app identifies you any more. The following are kept:

  • Hashed identifiers. One-way SHA-256 hashes (or keyed HMACs) of your phone number, Android device identifier, Google account identifier and tax ID, in the collections that enforce "one sign-up bonus, one referral bonus, and a limited number of cash-out accounts per person". The hash cannot be reversed to recover the original value; it can only be compared against a new sign-up. Without it, deleting and re-creating an account would earn the bonus again. Basis: legitimate interest in fraud prevention (LGPD Art. 7 IX and Art. 16 IV; Decreto 1377/2013 Art. 9).
  • Your identity, sealed for 12 months. Your email, name and Google account identifier, with the deletion date, are moved to a restricted store that no user, and none of our normal systems, can read. They exist only so that a court order concerning a connection record from the last year can still be answered with the person behind it, as Marco Civil Art. 13 requires, and they are purged automatically 12 months after deletion — the same moment the last connection record from your account expires. Basis: legal obligation (LGPD Art. 7 II and Art. 16 I).
  • Access and connection records for the remainder of their 12-month period (legal obligation).
  • Session totals, ledger entries and payout records, which reference your account ID, for accounting and dispute purposes (LGPD Art. 16 I and III).
  • The anonymised profile record, which exists only so the records above have a stable account to refer to.

Everything else about you is deleted. If you ask us to erase your data (section 10), our reply will list exactly which of the items above we are keeping and why.

9. HOW DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION SAFE?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest on Google Cloud. Phone numbers, device identifiers, tax IDs and payout details are stored in restricted locations that other users can never read, and that our own systems access only through server-side code. Access to connection records by staff is logged. Chat messages are end-to-end encrypted with keys that never leave your device. Anti-fraud identifiers are stored only as one-way hashes. No system is perfectly secure; if we discover a breach that is likely to cause you harm we will notify the ANPD or the SIC and you within the periods required by law.

10. WHAT ARE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS?

Wherever you are, you can ask us to confirm whether we process your data, to access it, correct it, receive a copy in a portable format, delete it, or object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Send requests to privacy@bumpapp.xyz from the email address on your account, or through the in-app feedback reporter while signed in so we can verify it is you.

Brazil (LGPD)

Under Art. 18 you have the right to: confirmation that we process your data; access; correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data; anonymisation, blocking or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data or data processed in breach of the LGPD; portability to another provider; deletion of data processed on the basis of consent; information about the public and private entities we share data with; information about the possibility of refusing consent and its consequences; and revocation of consent. We answer in simplified form immediately where we can, and with a complete declaration within 15 days (Art. 19). You may also lodge a complaint with the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD) at gov.br/anpd.

Colombia (Ley 1581)

Under Art. 8 you have the right to know, update and rectify your data; to request proof of the authorisation you gave; to be informed how your data has been used; to file complaints with the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC); to revoke your authorisation and request deletion where no legal or contractual duty requires us to keep the data; and to access your data free of charge. Consultas (requests to know what we hold) are answered within 10 business days of receipt, extendable once by 5 business days with notice. Reclamos (correction, deletion, complaints about a breach of this policy) are answered within 15 business days of a complete claim, extendable once by 8 business days with notice; if a claim is incomplete we will ask you to complete it within 5 business days, and it lapses after 2 months without reply. Requests go to privacy@bumpapp.xyz; you may also contact the SIC at sic.gov.co.

Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw consent for location by revoking the permission in Android settings, and for everything else by deleting your account or writing to us. Withdrawal does not affect processing that already happened, nor processing we carry out on another basis (sections 4 and 8).

Account information

You can change your username, avatar and phone number in the app, download a copy of your data by asking us, and delete your account from Settings. Deletion is described in section 8.
