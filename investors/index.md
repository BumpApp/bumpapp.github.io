Bump is an Android app that lets people sell their spare mobile data to people nearby, and lets others buy low-cost Internet by the megabyte. This page collects the facts investors and journalists usually ask for.

## At a glance

- **Product:** peer-to-peer mobile Internet sharing, with phone-to-phone chat that works without Internet. [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=xyz.bumpapp.prod).
- **Status:** public beta on Android.
- **Markets:** Brazil and Colombia.
- **How people pay:** prepaid credits bought with Pix, PSE or card, from R$4 in Brazil and 10,000 COP in Colombia. Credits never expire.
- **How sharers are paid:** cash out by Pix in Brazil or Bre-B in Colombia.
- **Company:** Bump, Inc., Boston, United States. Details on the [Bump Inc](/about/) page.

## The market

Over 1 billion people own a mobile phone but have no or limited connectivity. Prepaid users pay 13x more per MB than postpaid customers, 1 GB of data costs more than 5% of average monthly income in low and middle income countries, and 84% of broadband there is mobile-only. The [mission](/mission/) page has the full picture, and the prices pages for [Brazil](/prices/brazil/) and [Colombia](/prices/colombia/) have the country numbers and carrier-by-carrier price comparisons with sources.
