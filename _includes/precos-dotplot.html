{%- comment -%} Dot plot of R$/GB for every pack in _data/precos_br.yml, small and monthly packs as two groups, with Bump's ceiling and Wi-Fi rate as reference lines. Params: lang ("pt" or "en"). Rows are sorted by R$/GB via zero-padded string keys, since Liquid can't sort on a computed value.
