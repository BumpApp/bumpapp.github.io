2. THE BUMP NETWORK

Bump lets a user who has an internet connection (a "Sharer") make it available to nearby users who do not (a "Client"). The Client's phone connects to the Sharer's phone over Wi-Fi Direct or Bluetooth, opens an encrypted VPN tunnel to a server operated by Bump, and reaches the internet through that server. The Sharer's phone relays the encrypted tunnel; it cannot see the Client's traffic, and the Client's traffic does not leave from the Sharer's own IP address. A user can be both at once.

  • Sharing. When you share, you allow other users' encrypted traffic to pass through your device and your internet connection, up to the limits you set in the app. You are responsible for making sure your mobile or broadband plan allows this (for example, that tethering is permitted) and for any charges your carrier applies. You can stop sharing at any time.
  • Using a connection. When you connect through a Sharer you pay for the data you use with credits (section 4). Availability, speed and coverage depend entirely on which users are nearby and sharing; we do not guarantee that a hotspot will be available, that it will stay connected, or that it will reach any particular speed.
  • Our servers. All Client traffic exits from Bump VPN servers. We keep connection records (which device, which server, when, how much data) for 12 months as Brazilian law requires, and we do not log the destinations you access. See Privacy Policy section 2.
  • Not a telecommunications provider. Bump does not own or operate the underlying mobile or broadband networks. Sharers are not our agents or employees, and we do not control when or where they share.
